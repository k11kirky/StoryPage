StoryPage

Only needed for "Publish now". Your team's StoryPage admin can give you these.

Choose what developers and content editors get. Pick the page sections editors should build pages from; the components they're made of come along automatically. Choices save as you go.

Then publish your library as usual (Assets → Publish library). StoryPage builds what changed in Storybook and Contentful, using your publish description as the release notes, and posts progress in Slack.
